🌙 The story

It's 7:45 p.m. at the City Mall. You and your team are here for the midnight film when every door locks. On the floor you find a note:

“Four rooms. Four codes. In each room, only the WH questions have the correct numbers. Find them, open the doors, and escape before the film ends.”

  1. 1 Read the eight question tickets in each room.
  2. 2 Select the five WH questions. Their numbers make the code.
  3. 3 Enter the code, complete the team mission and move on.

Yes/No questions have numbers too — but they are decoys. You have 3 hints for the whole escape.
